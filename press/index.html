Short

Lockify Labs is an independent studio making calm, private, clutter-free apps and games for Android.

Long

Lockify Labs is a one-person independent studio run by Leo. It makes the apps and games he wants to use himself: calm, private and free of clutter. Its first app, Lockify, is an encrypted file vault that keeps photos, videos and documents on the phone and can hide behind a working calculator. Its first game, Neon Mania, is a colour-mixing light puzzle with 200 levels across ten skies.

Short

Neon Mania is a calm puzzle game about light: rotate tiles to lead each colour home, cross routes without touching, and mix colours into new ones.

Long

In Neon Mania, every board starts scrambled. Each tap turns a tile a quarter turn, and light flows the moment a route connects. Lead blue, pink and yellow to their destinations, pass them over each other at crossings, split one beam two ways, and feed two colours into a Mixer to make violet, green, orange and, at last, white. 200 levels climb through ten chapters, from Dawn to Starlight, each with its own sky and music. Every level's fewest-moves solution is proven by the studio's own solver, so matching it earns a Perfect.

Short

Lockify is a private, encrypted file vault for Android. Files are encrypted before they reach storage and stay on the phone.

Long

Lockify keeps photos, videos and documents in an encrypted vault on the phone, organised in folders and viewable without leaving the app. Calculator Stealth Mode disguises it as a working calculator that opens the vault only after a secret PIN. Premium adds Disguise Mode, a separate Decoy Vault, Motion Lock and encrypted backup to the user's own Google Drive.
