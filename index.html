The studio

Fewer things, done properly.

01

Calm by default

No noise and no dark patterns. Clear screens, gentle sounds, and every one of them can be switched off.

02

Honest about data

Lockify keeps your files on your phone. Each app collects only what it needs, explains it in plain words, and never sells it.

03

Checked, not guessed

Every Neon Mania level is proven solvable by our own solver, with its fewest moves worked out exactly. Updates are tested on real phones.
