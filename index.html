Privacy Policy

App: Neon Mania  ·  Last updated: October 4, 2026

Neon Mania ("the App") is developed by Lockify Labs. This policy explains what information the App collects, and how it is used.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. No account registration is required. No data is sent to any server. All game progress is stored locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or crash reporting tools.

Device Permissions

The App requests the VIBRATE permission solely to provide haptic feedback during gameplay. No data is read or transmitted as a result of this permission.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If the App is updated to include new features that affect data collection (such as analytics or advertisements), this policy will be updated accordingly before such features are released.
