Privacy Policy

App: Neon Mania  ·  Last updated: October 4, 2026

Neon Mania ("the App") is developed by Lockify Labs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App collects, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • No account is needed, and we never ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your game progress, stars, items and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The App shows ads (Google AdMob), measures usage (Google Analytics for Firebase) and reports crashes (Firebase Crashlytics). These Google services collect device and usage data as described below.
  • In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information Stored on Your Device

Level progress, best scores, stars, lives, items (hints, solutions, extra moves, extra time), tutorial progress, Daily Gift progress and your settings (music, sound effects, vibration) are saved locally on your device. We do not upload them to our own servers. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes them.

Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The App uses the following services, all provided by Google. Each collects information automatically when you use the App and processes it under Google's own privacy policy.

Google AdMob (advertising)

AdMob shows ads in the App, including optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for free items. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, it may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate your approximate location), device model, operating system, language, app version and your interactions with ads.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy Settings in the App. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.

Google Analytics for Firebase (usage measurement)

Analytics helps us understand how the App is used so we can improve levels and features. It may collect an app instance ID, the advertising ID, device model, operating system, country, app version, session lengths, levels played and purchase events. It does not collect your name or email.

Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting)

When the App crashes or hits a serious error, Crashlytics sends a crash report so we can fix the problem. A report contains the error details, device model, operating system, app version, device state at the time of the crash (such as free memory and orientation) and an installation identifier.

Google Play Billing (purchases)

Purchases such as item packs, the Starter Pack and Neon Mania Plus are processed entirely by Google Play. Google handles your payment information; we never receive or store it. The App receives only purchase confirmations (product, order ID and purchase status) so it can deliver what you bought and restore one-time purchases such as Neon Mania Plus.

How We Use Information

  • To show ads and give you rewards for the ads you choose to watch.
  • To deliver and restore your purchases.
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs.
  • To understand which levels and features players enjoy, and improve the game.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse.

Your Choices

  • Ad personalization: change your consent from Settings → Ad Privacy Settings in the App. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Google ad settings: manage how Google personalizes ads at My Ad Center.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the App or clear its storage in your device's settings.
  • Data requests: since the App has no accounts, we cannot link analytics or crash data to you by name. If you would like us to delete data associated with your installation, contact us at the address below and we will help.

Data Retention and Security

Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for a limited period under Firebase's retention settings and then deleted. All data sent by these services is encrypted in transit.

Device Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads, send analytics and crash reports, and connect to Google Play for purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob and Analytics as described above.
  • Google Play Billing: to make in-app purchases.
  • Vibrate: to provide haptic feedback during gameplay. No data is read or sent because of this permission.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised. Significant changes will be reflected here before they take effect in the App.
