How it plays

Light goes where you lead it.

Every level starts scrambled. Each tap turns one tile a quarter turn, and the light follows the moment a route connects. New ideas arrive one at a time, each with a short guided tutorial.

Rotate

Turn the pieces until the track runs from the Start to the Destination of the same colour.

Cross

Two colours can share a crossing and pass straight over each other without mixing.

Split

A splitter sends one beam two ways, so a single Start can light up two Destinations.

Mix

Feed two colours into a Mixer and a new one comes out. Plan which colours meet, and where.

Questions

Good to know

When can I play?

Neon Mania is coming soon to Google Play. Check the news page, or come back here; the button above will change to a download link on launch day.

Is it free?

Yes, it is free to play. You can watch an optional ad for extra items, there are occasional ads between levels, and item packs are available to buy. Neon Mania Plus is a one-time purchase that removes the ads between levels.

Which phones does it run on?

Android phones and tablets running Android 11 or newer. An iPhone version is not planned yet.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. Neon Mania is made for all ages. Younger players never see personalised ads, and children under 13 only see ads suitable for them. The privacy policy has the details.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed, and the levels play offline. Ads, purchases and the optional Google Play Games cloud save need a connection.

I'm stuck or found a bug. Who do I tell?

Write to support@lockifylabs.com, or use Settings → Send Feedback in the game, which fills in your phone and game version for us.
